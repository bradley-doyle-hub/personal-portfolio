Four 3D explainer videos for ISED, made at Banfield. They introduced Innovative Solutions Canada's new bidding website to two audiences: inventors looking for funding and government departments looking for solutions. The videos are on YouTube.

## Two streams, four videos

The series covered two funding streams, Challenge and Testing. Each stream has one video for innovators and one for government departments. All four are on YouTube.

## Starting as support

I joined early as support. My co-workers handled character design, art direction and character rigging. I built 3D models and sets. At one point I was asked to model a few character designs in 3D, so the client had options to choose from.

## Building the sets

Once production started, I modelled, textured and rigged the sets and props for the videos. The work ran in Cinema 4D and Redshift.

## Taking it to the end

As the project went on, I took on more of it. By the end I was the main person on it, covering animation, rendering, editing and post-production. It ran about a year and a half, early in my 3D career.
