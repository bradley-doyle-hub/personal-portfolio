A playful, over-the-top Apple-style product animation of the LTT Screwdriver, made on my own from concept to final frame. It is a personal project, not made for or with LTT. It was shown on the WAN Show, and took my YouTube channel to about 300 subscribers.

One person, start to finish: concept, modelling, animation, edit and sound.

## The idea

This began as a way to learn Cinema 4D and hard-surface modelling. It was my first personal 3D project. I watch a lot of LTT videos and kept hearing about the screwdriver, and it had modelling challenges that suited what I wanted to learn. I had joked about an over-the-top Apple-style animation for it. The more I built the model, the more I wanted to make the film.

## What I explored

I modelled the whole screwdriver in Cinema 4D from photos and screenshots, since I did not own one. The tri-lobe handle was the first hard part: it had to look right and wrap around the handle. Animation was the second. I tried Unreal Engine, then finished in Blender, through many versions. I nearly skipped the assembly shot because it meant modelling the interior with no good reference. The film felt unfinished without it, so I built it.

## What I took from it

I modelled this about three years before I finished the film. I would not let a project sit that long again. It gave me a lot of practice in 3D and made me confident in how I create, and it changed my pipeline a lot.
