An abstract 3D explainer for the cybersecurity company eSentire, made in 2022 with Cloud in the Sky. The goal was to show what eSentire does and who they are without walking through the product. I was art director and 3D lead, and I edited it.

## A brief that changed

The first idea was blocks moving around with stock footage projected onto them. After a few conversations with the creative director, we built a visual moodboard together and the direction changed to what you see here.

## Data as blocks in a warehouse

The hardest part was finding visuals for a cybersecurity company. We landed on data shown as blocks in a warehouse. Mondrian-style recursive subdivision builds much of the imagery, with Houdini handling some of the subdivision and Cinema 4D and Redshift doing the rest.

## Built around the voice-over

The voice-over came first, and the piece was built to make sense of it, then iterated until it hit the mark. Many scenes and shots changed or were cut along the way.
